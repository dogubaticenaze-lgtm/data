Scope of the Power of Attorney

I hereby authorise the attorney named above to carry out, in my name and on my behalf, the following acts and formalities in connection with the repatriation of the remains of the deceased identified above:

  1. To request and collect the death certificate, the multilingual death certificate extract (Formule C), the burial transit permit and the embalming certificate from the hospital, municipality, civil registry office and any other competent authority;
  2. To conduct consular formalities in Turkey and abroad and to obtain the approvals and documents required for the repatriation;
  3. To collect the remains of the deceased from the hospital, mortuary or forensic medicine institution;
  4. To make airline cargo bookings, to have the air waybill (AWB) issued and to complete customs formalities;
  5. To sign, submit to the competent authorities and receive all declarations, applications, forms and reports required for these formalities;
  6. To pay any fees and charges arising from the above formalities and to receive the related receipts and documents.

This power of attorney is limited to the repatriation formalities described above and remains valid until those formalities have been completed.
