Repatriation Document Checklist

Documents required for the international repatriation of human remains. Tick each box as the document is obtained.

A) Repatriation from Turkey to another country

Document Issued by / Notes
☐Death certificateHospital or municipal physician, by place of death
☐Multilingual death certificate extract (Formule C)Civil registry office (Nüfus Müdürlüğü), after registration
☐Burial transit permit (cenaze yol izin belgesi)Municipality (cemeteries directorate / municipal physician)
☐Embalming certificatePhysician or institution that carried out the embalming
☐Sealed (zinc-lined) coffin reportMunicipal physician / embalmer, when the coffin is closed
☐Consular repatriation approval from the destination countryConsulate general in Istanbul or embassy in Ankara of the destination country
☐Air waybill (AWB)Airline cargo; issued after the booking is confirmed
☐Public prosecutor's burial permit (forensic cases only)Chief public prosecutor's office; after the post-mortem
☐Passport / identity document of the deceasedNext of kin; the original is required during transport
☐Insurance policy or funeral plan membership details (if any)Next of kin / insurer; for cover of costs
☐Receiving person / funeral director detailsFuneral director or next of kin at destination: name, telephone, address

B) Repatriation from another country to Turkey

Document Issued by / Notes
☐Death certificate issued abroad, with translationLocal registrar; sworn translation, apostille where required
☐Turkish consular repatriation certificate (cenaze nakil belgesi)Turkish consulate in the country where the death occurred
☐Embalming certificateFuneral director or physician abroad
☐Sealed coffin certificateFuneral director abroad
☐Air waybill (AWB)Airline cargo
☐Municipal burial permit in TurkeyMunicipality / cemeteries directorate at the place of burial
☐Turkish identity card / passport of the deceasedNext of kin
☐Cemetery details for the burialNext of kin; cemeteries directorate for grave allocation
This checklist is for general information only; additional documents may be required depending on the destination country. Where death was caused by a contagious disease (cholera, plague, smallpox, anthrax, leprosy, glanders), Turkish regulations prohibit repatriation for one year. Cremation is not available in Turkey.
